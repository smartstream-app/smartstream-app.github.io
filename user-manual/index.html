What is SmartStream?

An Android TV and Fire TV app that turns your own IPTV account into a living-room experience: movies and series with posters, Live TV with an on-screen guide, Continue Watching, household profiles, and optional AI search. Favorite channels can carry backup streams from other providers, and Intelligent Switching moves between them automatically when a stream fails — something no other IPTV app does. You need your own IPTV provider — SmartStream does not sell content.

Remote control

  • D-pad — move focus
  • OK — select
  • Back — go back one step (does not exit the app from Home)
  • Hold OK on a movie/series card — rating (👍/👎)
  • Live TV fullscreen: Up/Down zap channels, Left opens the guide overlay, Right jumps to the previous channel
