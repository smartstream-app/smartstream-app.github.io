Media Dashboard

The left menu is Home, Movies, Series, and Live TV. Your IPTV account becomes a living-room dashboard — channels, films, and shows in one place.

  • ✓Live TV, movies and series: Open the matching menu and start watching.
  • ✓Several providers: If you connect more than one, Intelligent Switching moves to another stream when the current one fails or a slot is taken. No other IPTV app does this.

Home

Stop scrolling through endless, messy lists. Home puts Continue Watching, suggestions, and My List first — then you jump to Movies, Series, or Live TV.

  • ✓Continue Watching: Resume movies and queue up next episodes instantly.
  • ✓AI Suggestions: Tailored rows of movies and series based on your taste.
  • ✓Personal Watchlists: Bookmark titles to 'My List' for quick access later.

Live TV that stays on

Watch live channels with an on-screen guide (EPG), instant preview, and fullscreen without reconnecting. Connect up to 5 providers and pick the channel folders you actually use.

  • ✓Favorite channels with multiple streams: Link backup streams from other providers onto the same favorite channel. If one source is busy or drops, another is already attached.
  • ✓Intelligent Switching between providers: When playback fails or another device takes the slot, SmartStream switches automatically. No other IPTV app does this.
  • ✓Guide, favorites and catch-up: EPG grid, Favorite Channels and PPV, reminders, and catch-up when your provider supports it.

Movies and series

Poster grids with descriptions, filters, and a details screen. Choose exactly which folders to import from each provider.

  • ✓Browse: Genre, language, and sort — saved per profile.
  • ✓Details: Play, resume, trailer, audio, subtitles, and stream quality.
  • ✓Your taste: Rate titles so suggestions get better.
  • ✓Intelligent Switching between providers: When playback fails or another device takes the slot, SmartStream switches automatically. No other IPTV app does this.

Search — type or speak

Your library actually learns from you. By connecting a free Google Gemini API key, the app analyzes your 👍 and 👎 ratings to generate dedicated Suggested Movies and Suggested Series rows.

Skip typing on the remote entirely. Press the microphone and speak naturally: "A thrilling 90s crime drama" or "A zombie movie with dark humor." AI understands your intent and finds exact matches inside your own personal catalog.

Android TV Home Screen Channels

You don't even need to open the app to decide what to watch tonight. SmartStream integrates deeply with your television operating system.

Publish custom native channels directly onto your main Android TV / Google TV home page. Add dedicated launcher rows for each of your profiles—such as Continue Watching, AI Suggested Movies, and AI Suggested Series.

Built for the Whole Household

Keep everyone's viewing habits separate with up to 5 isolated profiles. Every person gets their own individual watch history, custom AI recommendations, and personal watchlist.

  • ✓Kids Profiles: Create safe spaces restricted strictly to approved folders.
  • ✓PIN Protection: Lock mature or sensitive category folders behind a PIN.

A new TV in under two minutes

Cloud Sync lets you install SmartStream on another device in under two minutes. Providers, profiles, and watch history arrive ready to use — you don't start from scratch.

Everything stored in the cloud is encrypted. Your settings and library stay private in your own Google Drive.

  • ✓Under 2 minutes: New TV, same household.
  • ✓Encrypted in the cloud: Data is encrypted before it leaves the TV. Only your household PIN can unlock it.
